## A topic the data says we are missing
**What happens if you violate federal pretrial release** was one of the strongest phrases in the whole pull (2 engines, 47 distinct queries), with "violation of pretrial release conditions" and "pretrial release violation report" close behind. The book touches it only in passing (a halfway house rule-break can land you in jail and revoke bail, p. 144). Worth its own article if Bilal has more to say.
