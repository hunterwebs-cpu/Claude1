**Heading:** Computer Monitoring Program Participant Agreement
**Section heading:** General Provisions

> I, ______________________, have been ______ in the Computer Monitoring Program as ordered by the Court. I agree to comply with all program rules set forth in this agreement, and the instructions of my probation officer. I agree to install, or allow to be installed, at my own expense, equipment or software to monitor my approved computer or internet ready device.
>
> 2. I shall notify the U.S. Probation Office, in advance, of any computer(s), connected device(s), internet capable devices and/or gaming devices I own or have access to. I shall notify the probation officer, in advance, of any additional computer/connected device(s) I acquire or have access to during the course of my supervision.

> 3. I shall not use, own, or access any device which allows internet access other than what I have been authorized to use by the Probation Office. > 4. I understand that I will complete the Computer/Internet Data Form and return it within seven (7) days.
>
> 5. While using my monitored device, I shall not use any form of "in-private" browsing at any time.

>
> 7. I agree not to attempt to remove, tamper with, reverse engineer, or in any way circumvent the monitoring software and/or hardware.
>
> 8. I understand that I am prohibited from using removable, bootable media to boot any computer to any operating system.
>
> 9. I understand that I will not use any encrypted email accounts.
>
> 10. I understand that I am prohibited from creating, posting, and/or using misleading profiles.
>
> 11. I shall not access any Internet Service Provider account or other online service using someone else's account, name, designation, or alias.
>
> 12. I shall not utilize any service that conceals or spoofs my Internet Protocol address.
>
> 13. I shall not have another individual access the Internet on my behalf to obtain files or information unless pre-approved by the probation officer.
