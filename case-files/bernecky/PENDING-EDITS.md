## Notes on Part II, post-8-item-edit read-through

1. **Ambiguous pronoun "he," and courts are never referred to by pronoun
   at all.** Flagged passage: "...treatment condition he entered is Mr.
   Bernecky's Special Condition..." Client: "who is 'he'? and you never
   ever refer to a court as such anyway. it is either the court or name
   the judge." Two problems: (a) as written, "he" is genuinely ambiguous
   about who is meant; (b) as a matter of style/practice, a court/judge
   should never be referred to by a pronoun — always either "the Court"
   or the judge's actual name (e.g., "Judge Vilardo"). Sweep the
   surrounding paragraphs for any other pronoun standing in for a judge or
   the Court and fix all of them, not just this one instance.
