   only as quoted inside *Kunz*, *Browder*, and *Carlineo*.
2. **United States v. Mike**, 632 F.3d 686 (10th Cir. 2011) — the case the
   existing motion's Part I.A (employer) argument currently relies on. Failed

   tools/sources tried each time. Given the reframing direction below, this needs
   resolution before Part I.A is redrafted.

   the most directly relevant SORA precedent found, and its Monroe County origin

   tried; several mirrors also failed. This is a document, not a case, but it's

## HIGH PRIORITY — NY SORA track

5. **People v. Gillotti**, 23 N.Y.3d 841 (2014) — sets the departure
   burden-of-proof standard (preponderance, not clear-and-convincing) that
   everything else in the SORA research depends on.
6. **People v. Johnson**, 11 N.Y.3d 416 (2008) — origin of the Factor 3/7
   "excessive registration" reasoning for CP offenders.
7. **People v. Gunter**, 2023 NY Slip Op 03247 (2d Dep't June 14, 2023) — closest
   adverse analog to Bernecky's override-based departure argument.
8. **People v. Butler**, 2024 (2d Dep't, Suffolk County, Docket No. 2022-10253,
   Nov. 13, 2024) — near-identical fact pattern (CP-type override + CP-type
   instant offense + reoffense during treatment); no confirmed NY Slip Op number.
9. **People v. Stagles**, 2023 NY Slip Op 06613 (4th Dep't Dec. 22, 2023) — the
   one favorable, controlling-department (Fourth) departure grant found; read only
   via a third-party case digest, not the decision itself.
10. **People v. Adams**, 2023 NY Slip Op 02813 — two research passes produced
    **conflicting** characterizations (department, county, and outcome all
    disputed between them) — needs definitive resolution.
11. **The June 1, 2012 Board Position Statement** referenced in Bernecky's own
    Case Summary — not a case, but the single highest-value document specific to
    this matter. Research suggests (via *People v. Marrero*) it may actually be
    the Board's Factor 3/7 scoring statement, not a general-dangerousness
    statement, misapplied here to justify the Level III override finding.
