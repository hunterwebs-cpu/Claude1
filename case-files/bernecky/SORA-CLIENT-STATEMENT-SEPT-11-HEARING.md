# Verbal Objection to Be Read Into the Record by Jeffrey Bernecky

**Hearing:** People v. Bernecky, SORA Risk-Level Classification Hearing
**September 11, 2026, 11:30 a.m., Hon. Karen Bailey Turner, J.C.C., Monroe County Court**

---

Your Honor, before we go any further, I want to state an objection for the record: this hearing cannot proceed fairly today, and I want to explain why — because it is not for lack of trying on my part.

I prepared six documents in advance of this hearing: an Application for Assignment of Counsel, a Motion to Adjourn, a Demand for Disclosure and Subpoena Duces Tecum, a Motion for Forensic Expert Funding under County Law § 722-c, a Memorandum of Law, and my own Certification with Exhibits A through E. I tried to file them with the Clerk's Office myself and was told the Court does not accept filings from someone who isn't represented. I called the Public Defender's Office to get a lawyer. Whether an attorney has been assigned to this case on paper, I do not know, and it does not matter — no attorney from that office has taken my call, reviewed my documents, or spoken with me about this case at all. A name on a file is not representation. I called the Clerk's Office a second time before today's hearing to try again, and I was refused again, on the same grounds.

I asked for a lawyer in writing. I called for one. I tried to file my own papers, twice. None of it worked, and none of it failed because I sat on my hands. I am standing here today with no attorney who has ever spoken to me and with six documents that have never reached this Court's record, for reasons that have nothing to do with my own effort.

A hearing that decides my risk level, and how the state notifies my community about me, is not something I should face without a lawyer and without my own materials ever having reached the Court — not when I did everything available to me to prevent exactly that. That is what makes today unfair, and that is my objection.

I am asking the Court for three things. First, accept these six documents into the record now, since I have exhausted every other way of filing them. Second, adjourn this hearing. Correction Law § 168-n(3) says the Court "shall adjourn" where there is a dispute over the Board's determinations and materials still need to be obtained — not "may adjourn" — and my Demand for Disclosure identifies exactly that dispute: both federal presentence reports the Board's case summary relies on, the Board's 2012 position statement, the judgments of conviction from 2012 and 2018, polygraph-related materials, and communications between the Board and probation about me. None of that is in hand. Third, assign me a lawyer who will actually represent me — not a warm body standing next to me at counsel table, and not just a name I am told has been entered on a file somewhere, but an attorney who will actually have time, before any new hearing date, to read what I prepared, meet with me, and decide with me how to proceed. That is what happened instead at my last SORA hearing in this courthouse: assigned counsel appeared the day of the hearing with no chance to prepare, and later filed an appeal on my case without my knowledge or my consent.

I am not asking for a delay to avoid this case. I am asking the Court to recognize that I have done my part, and that the reasons I am standing here without a lawyer and without a filed record are not of my own making. Thank you, Your Honor.
