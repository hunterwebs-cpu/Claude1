# Statement to Be Read Into the Record by Jeffrey Bernecky

**Hearing:** People v. Bernecky, SORA Risk-Level Classification Hearing
**September 11, 2026, 11:30 a.m., Hon. Karen Bailey Turner, J.C.C., Monroe County Court**

---

Your Honor, my name is Jeffrey Bernecky. I am the respondent in this case, and before we go any further today I want to put a few things on the record.

I prepared six documents in advance of this hearing: an Application for Assignment of Counsel, a Motion to Adjourn, a Demand for Disclosure and Subpoena Duces Tecum, a Motion for Forensic Expert Funding under County Law § 722-c, a Memorandum of Law, and my own Certification with Exhibits A through E. I tried to file all of them with the Clerk's office myself. I was told the Court does not accept filings from someone who isn't represented, and that I should either go through an attorney or bring them here today. I am asking the Court to accept these documents now, or to direct that they be filed as soon as counsel is assigned, so that what I prepared is actually part of the record the Court considers.

Second, I am asking for an adjournment. Correction Law § 168-n(3) says the Court "shall adjourn" this hearing where there is a dispute over the Board's determinations and materials still need to be obtained — not "may adjourn." My Demand for Disclosure identifies exactly that dispute: both federal presentence reports the Board's case summary relies on, the Board's own 2012 position statement, the judgments of conviction from 2012 and 2018, polygraph-related materials, and communications between the Board and probation about me. None of that is in hand yet. I am asking the Court to adjourn this hearing to a date that actually allows those materials to be produced and reviewed, and allows whoever is assigned to represent me time to prepare, consistent with the motion I am filing today.

Third, and most importantly, I am asking for a lawyer who will actually represent me — not just stand next to me. At my last SORA hearing in this courthouse, assigned counsel appeared the day of the hearing, with no chance beforehand to review my file or talk to me, and later filed an appeal on my case without my knowledge or my consent. I am not asking for anything unusual. I am asking that whoever is assigned to me this time have the time, before the hearing, to read what I have prepared, meet with me, and decide with me how to proceed — and that if that lawyer is not willing or able to do that, the Court hear about it directly, rather than have me stand here again with a lawyer in name only.

I am not trying to delay this case indefinitely, and I am not trying to make this harder than it has to be. I am asking for what the statute already promises me: real notice of the case against me, real time to answer it, and a lawyer who will actually do that with me. Thank you, Your Honor.
