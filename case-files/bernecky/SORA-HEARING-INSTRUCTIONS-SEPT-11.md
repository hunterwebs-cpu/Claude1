## PART 1 — What to Say if a Lawyer Appears (hand over the packet as you say this)

"I have been trying to reach a lawyer, and trying to file these documents with the Court, ever since I was notified of this hearing in late July. I do not believe it is possible for you to represent me today — you have never spoken with me, you know nothing about my case, you have not sought any discovery, and you have not reviewed anything I have prepared. For this hearing, I am asking you to tell the Judge that we need an adjournment, and that I intend to contest the proposed Level Three classification to the fullest extent of the law.

Here is a summary of the argument I have prepared, so you understand what is at stake even without having had time to review it: my Risk Assessment Instrument score is 70 points — within Level One under the Guidelines' own table. I am being pushed to a presumptive Level Three only because of a single override that fires automatically from the bare fact of my 2012 conviction — the same fact that would support a Level Two classification, which I do not contest. The Board has never independently shown that I present a danger to anyone's physical safety; both of my convictions are entirely internet-based, with no violence, no weapon, and no physical contact with a child alleged in either one. The Board's case for Level Three rests on its own paraphrase of a federal presentence report it has never produced, and I dispute several of the specific statements that paraphrase attributes to me. All of this is set out, with citations to the record, in the Memorandum of Law and Certification I am handing you now."

## PART 2 — What to Say to the Judge

"Your Honor, this Court notified me of this hearing in late July. Since that day, I have tried to reach a lawyer, and I have tried to file documents with this Court myself. Both efforts failed through no lack of trying on my part.

I believe this proceeding cannot go forward fairly today. I am told, on one hand, that I am entitled to a lawyer. On the other hand, whoever fills that role today has never spoken with me, knows nothing about my case, has sought no discovery, and has not reviewed anything I have prepared. A lawyer in name only is not the representation the law promises me.

I am asking the Court for three things. First, an adjournment, so that a lawyer who will actually represent me has time to review my case and prepare. Second, that the documents I have brought with me today — a Memorandum of Law, my own Certification with Exhibits, a Motion to Adjourn, an Application for Assignment of Counsel, a Motion for Forensic Expert Funding, and a Demand for Disclosure — be accepted into the record; I have been trying to file them since late July and have not been able to. Third, real, assigned counsel — not someone standing next to me who has never opened my file.

I am not asking to delay this case to avoid it. I intend to contest the proposed Level Three classification to the fullest extent of the law, and I am prepared to do that the moment I have a lawyer who can actually help me do it. Thank you, Your Honor."
