Ordinarily, this court does not consider pro se motions filed
by represented defendants. See United States v.- Tracy, 989 F.2d
1279, 1285 (lst Cir. 1993) ("A district court enjoys wide latitude
in managing its docket and can require represented parties to
present motions through counsel."); United States v. Lépez-Soto,
960 F.3d 1, 15 (1st Cir. 2020) ("So long as (defendant] was
represented by counsel, he had no right to file his own motions").
