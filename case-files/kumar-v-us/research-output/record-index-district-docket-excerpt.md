## What this excerpt establishes (and the discrepancies it raises)

1. **Kumar was represented by appointed counsel (Charles Fasoldt) from 3/15/2022 onward on this docket**, not "pro se with standby counsel." Kumar's brief (Br. 16, 51) says that when he filed the motion to dismiss he was "pro se with standby counsel" (true for 2/28/2022) and that he "maintained it through a Faretta hearing when the government pressed him to accept representation by counsel who would not pursue it." The clerk's notes (ECF 65) show how that hearing ended: he chose counsel, Fasoldt was appointed (previously standby counsel, ECF 53), and he asked that his pro se motions still be decided. The brief does not say that he was represented thereafter. Judge Wolf's 4/26/2022 order (ECF 72) required Kumar to consult Fasoldt about whether to maintain the motion. **Implications:** (a) The withdrawal in Sept. 2022 (ECF 83 and the docket entries ECF 78-85, unavailable) probably occurred while Fasoldt represented him -- this makes an IAC-induced-withdrawal theory (Ground Two) legally viable (no Faretta n.46 problem) *if* Fasoldt (or whoever was counsel at the time) gave the 15-20 month estimate; (b) Kumar's brief's description of his status appears inaccurate and should be corrected rather than repeated -- an error of fact in a brief invites credibility attacks; (c) the Government cannot call it a pro se tactical choice without citing the actual docket. **Need ECF 78-85, 93 to confirm the status at withdrawal.**
2. **The Government opposed the motion to dismiss on the merits (ECF 64) and the court never ruled** (per the Government's motion and Kumar's brief, it was withdrawn).
3. The court itself (ECF 72) asked whether Kumar "wishes to maintain the Motion" -- i.e., the court was alert to the withdrawal decision. The Government will say the court's inquiry shows knowing waiver; we will say the inquiry did not address the Guidelines misestimate.
4. ECF 61: the Government itself moved for a Faretta colloquy -- possibly to ensure Kumar accepted counsel; shows the Government's awareness of the motion-to-dismiss dynamic.
5. The separate civil docket 1:24-cv-12710 bears the legend "DO NOT DOCKET IN THIS CASE - ALL ENTRIES ARE TO BE MADE IN CRIMINAL CASE" (CourtListener docket_id 69308265): confirms that the 2255 filings are ECF numbers in the criminal case (ECF 120 et seq.).
6. D.R.I. dockets exist on CourtListener (docket_ids 25152488, 18557674; No. 1:20-cr-00089; filed 10/21/2020; terminated 2/12/2021) but docket-entry retrieval returned no entries for 25152488; 18557674 not yet retrieved (rate limit).
