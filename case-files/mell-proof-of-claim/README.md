# In re S. Bradley Mell — Proof of Claim Research

**Matter:** In re S. Bradley Mell, Case No. 26-16834-EJO (Bankr. D.N.J.)
**Question:** Whether creditors Courtney Mell (~$200,000) and Diana Mell (~$80,000)
must file proofs of claim to protect their rights, where the chapter 7 trustee has
designated the case a no-asset case and stated no claim form is necessary.

## One-Line Answer

The trustee is right — no proof of claim is needed now in a no-asset chapter 7, and
Rule 3002(c)(5) guarantees a fresh 90-day window if assets ever surface — but the
memo identifies a separate, genuinely time-sensitive deadline (Rule 4007(c)
nondischargeability) that a claim form does not protect, plus a jury-waiver trap
(Langenkamp) if either creditor files a protective claim.
