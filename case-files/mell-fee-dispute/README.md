# Mell — Chapter 7 Fee Dispute (Internal Assessment)

**Matter:** In re Steven Bradley Mell, Ch. 7, U.S. Bankruptcy Court, D.N.J., Case No. 26-16834 (EOH)
**Firm on the disputed bill:** McManimon, Scotland & Baumann, LLC (MS&B)
**Posture:** Internal assessment only — is the client's reading of the flat-fee scope legally defensible under NJ law? No court filing.

## The question
The Chapter 7 retainer states a "flat" $5,000 fee and also provides for hourly billing.
- **Client's reading:** flat $5,000 covers work directly related to the Ch. 7 filing (petition/schedules,
  § 341 creditors' meeting, trustee conferences); only *ancillary* services are billed hourly.
- **Firm's apparent reading:** more work billed hourly (invoice INV 264503 charges hourly rates for
  work the client believed was within the flat fee).
- **Secondary issue:** some invoice line items appear dated *before* the retainer was executed.

A separate $20,000 retainer (dated June 25, 2026) covers MS&B's opposition to the "B.B. MTD"
(Nagel & Rice's motion to dismiss the petition); it states expressly it is **not** a flat fee. That
agreement is internally consistent and is not the focus of this dispute.
