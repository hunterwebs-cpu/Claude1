## What this means for the $2,658.40 "balance due"
- The **CH7‑FLAT** work ($1,842.50+) is the very filing work the paid $5,000 already bought — it should not appear as an hourly charge at all.
- The **MTD/ADV** work ($2,292.50) is not properly on this invoice; per the retainer it needed a **separate** agreement, and virtually all of it predates the 6/25 MTD retainer (and most predates the Ch. 7 retainer entirely).
- The **IMPROPER** entry ($37.50) is not chargeable.
- The **UNSPEC** entries ($2,185, almost all pre-execution strategy) can't be justified on their face and, being pre-retainer, are quantum-meruit-only at best.
- **Genuinely hourly Bucket 2 work ("settlement discussions with an adversary re: claims") on this invoice ≈ $0.**

**Bottom line:** Applying the client's reading (which tracks the retainer's own "in sum" clause), essentially **none** of the $7,320 is properly billable as hourly on top of the paid $5,000 flat fee. The legitimately owed amount is the **$338.40 in disbursements** (the real filing fee), with the possible exception of any post-6/25 adversary work that should have been billed on the $20K MTD retainer instead — of which this invoice shows none after that date.
